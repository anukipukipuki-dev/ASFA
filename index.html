❓ How to use this dashboard

This tool lets the integrator team track all the documentation required to integrate the onboard ASFA Digital system into a Vectron locomotive and obtain its entry-into-service authorisation in Spain.

Project workflow

Phase 0Baseline analysis and planning: technical and regulatory baseline.
Phase 1System design and technical documentation of the onboard integration.
Phase 2Workshop verification and test-bench trials.
Phase 3On-line trials over the RFIG (track testing).
Phase 4Authorisation documentation before AESF / ERA (OSS).
Phase 5Entry into service and post-authorisation documentation.

What the statuses mean

Pending — not started.
In Progress — being drafted or prepared.
Under Review — drafted, awaiting internal or external validation.
Completed — finalized and approved.
Blocked — cannot proceed (external dependency, missing information, etc.).
Not Applicable — not required for this configuration/project.

Use the sidebar to navigate between phases, the filters to find specific documents, and the export buttons to back up your progress or generate a printable report.
